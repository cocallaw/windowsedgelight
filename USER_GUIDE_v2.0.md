# Windows Edge Light v2.0 - User Guide

## 🎉 What's New in Version 2.0

Windows Edge Light now gives you complete control over your edge lighting with two powerful new features:

### 🌡️ Temperature Control
Adjust your light from warm candlelight to cool daylight.

### 📌 Smart Presets
Save and instantly recall your three favorite lighting setups.

---

## 🌡️ Using Temperature Control

### What is Light Temperature?

Light temperature (measured in Kelvin) determines the color tone of your edge light:

| Temperature | Color Tone | Best For |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| **2700K** 🔥 | Warm Orange/Yellow | Evening, relaxation, warm ambiance |
| **4500K** ⚖️ | Neutral White | General use, balanced lighting |
| **6500K** ❄️ | Cool Blue/White | Daytime, focus, energetic feel |

### How to Adjust Temperature

**Option 1: Use the Slider** (Recommended)
1. Hover over the control panel at bottom center of your screen
2. Find the temperature slider (marked with 🔥 warm and ❄️ cool)
3. Drag the slider left for warmer light, right for cooler light
4. Watch the edge light change color in real-time!
5. Current temperature is shown (e.g., "5200K")

**Option 2: Use the System Tray**
1. Right-click the Windows Edge Light icon in your taskbar
2. Select "🌡️ Temperature: Warmer" or "🌡️ Temperature: Cooler"
3. Each click adjusts by 100K

### Your Setting is Saved Automatically
When you adjust the temperature, it's automatically saved. Next time you launch Windows Edge Light, your preferred temperature will be restored.

## 📌 Using Presets

### What are Presets?

Presets let you save three different lighting configurations, each with:
- ✅ A specific temperature (e.g., 3500K)
- ✅ A specific brightness level (e.g., 80%)

Perfect for:
- 🌅 **Morning preset**: Cool and bright for focus
- 🌆 **Afternoon preset**: Neutral and moderate  
- 🌙 **Evening preset**: Warm and dim for relaxation

### Default Presets (Ready to Use!)

Out of the box, you get:

| Preset | Name | Temperature | Brightness | Best For |
|--------|------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **1** | Cool & Bright | 6500K ❄️ | 100% | Daytime work, video calls |
| **2** | Neutral | 4500K ⚖️ | 70% | General computing |
| **3** | Warm & Cozy | 2700K 🔥 | 50% | Evening, relaxation |

### Loading a Preset

**Super Easy - Just Click!**
1. Hover over the control panel
2. Look for the three preset buttons labeled **1**, **2**, **3**
3. Click any button to instantly apply that preset
4. Both temperature and brightness change immediately

**From System Tray:**
1. Right-click the taskbar icon
2. Select "📌 Load Preset 1", "📌 Load Preset 2", or "📌 Load Preset 3"

### Saving Your Own Presets

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Adjust the temperature slider to your preference
2. Adjust brightness using the 🔅/🔆 buttons
3. Hold down **Ctrl** key
4. While holding Ctrl, click one of the preset buttons (1, 2, or 3)
5. You'll see a confirmation: "Preset X saved!"

**Example Workflow:**
```
Goal: Create a "Reading Mode" preset

1. Set temperature to 3200K (warm white)
2. Set brightness to 65%
3. Hold Ctrl + Click [2]
4. Confirmation appears
5. Done! Preset 2 now saves your reading mode
```

### Your Presets are Saved Forever
All three presets are automatically saved. They'll be there:
- ✅ After closing the app
- ✅ After restarting Windows
- ✅ After updates

| Shortcut | Action |

## 💡 Usage Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Video Conference Setup
**Goal**: Bright, professional lighting

1. Click Preset **1** (Cool & Bright)

   - Professional, energetic appearance

**Goal**: Low strain, immersive lighting

   - Sets 2700K warm at 50%
   - Reduces eye strain

**Goal**: Personal preference for afternoon work

1. Set temperature to 4200K (slightly warm neutral)
2. Set brightness to 75%
3. Hold Ctrl + Click Preset **2**
4. Now clicking 2 instantly recalls this setup

**Goal**: Match lighting to outdoor light

**Summer (bright sun):**
- Use higher temperatures (5500K-6500K)
- Higher brightness (80-100%)

**Winter (dim days):**
- Use lower temperatures (3000K-4000K)  
- Moderate brightness (50-70%)

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What temperature should I use for video calls?**
A: 5500K-6500K gives the most professional, energetic appearance. Try Preset 1.

**Q: Can I have more than three presets?**
A: Currently limited to three, but you can change them anytime by saving over existing ones.

**Q: Does temperature affect performance?**
A: No, it only changes the color. Performance is identical across all temperatures.

**Q: Will my settings transfer to a new computer?**
A: Settings are stored locally. You'll need to reconfigure on a new computer.

**Q: What if I don't see the control panel?**
A: Move your mouse to the bottom center of your screen. It appears when you hover.

**Q: Can I adjust temperature with keyboard shortcuts?**
A: Not directly, but you can use system tray menu or save frequently-used temperatures as presets.
